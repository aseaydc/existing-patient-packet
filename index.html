Beyond the Adjustment

There Is More Available to You Than You May Realize

Patients who have been coming in for adjustments sometimes do not realize the full scope of what is available at Seay Wellness. If any of the following sounds like something you or someone in your life could benefit from, just ask.

6-Month Program
M4 Transformation Program

A comprehensive six-month program combining chiropractic care with functional medicine. Designed for patients who want to address the root cause of chronic health challenges, not manage them visit by visit. Includes labs, nutrition guidance, and metabolic assessment.

For GLP-1 Medication Users
GLP-1 Cellular Restoration Program

A specialized recovery protocol for patients on Ozempic, Wegovy, or similar medications. Addresses muscle loss, nutrient depletion, and mitochondrial stress that often accompany rapid weight loss. More at support4glp1.com.

Standalone Service
Functional Medicine

Most patients see Dr. Seay for chiropractic care and never realize he also runs comprehensive functional medicine evaluations. If you are dealing with fatigue, hormone imbalance, gut issues, chronic inflammation, or a health problem no one has been able to explain, functional medicine starts with advanced lab work to find out exactly what is happening inside your body. No guesswork. No symptom management. Ask Dr. Seay if this is right for you.

You do not need a referral to explore any of these. Just mention it at your next visit and Dr. Seay will walk you through whether it is the right fit.
